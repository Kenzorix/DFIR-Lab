# FTK Imager
## Overview
FTK Imager is a digital forensics tool used to acquire, preview, and examine digital evidence.
## What I Learned
- Adding forensic evidence to FTK Imager
- Examining forensic images
- Navigating files and folders within evidence
- Examining file-system information
- Identifying deleted files
- Preserving and documenting forensic evidence
## Practical Work
I used FTK Imager to load and examine forensic evidence created during my DFIR laboratory exercises on my windows 10 inside virtualbox
